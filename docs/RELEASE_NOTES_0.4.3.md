# Field Trial Secretary v0.4.3 beta

Expands Best in Event management for multi-day and Grand Prix-style events and improves recovery from scoring mistakes.

- Compares hound totals across two or three selected trials and highlights the third-highest eligible hound.
- Adds printable BIE record sheets after scoring is complete.
- Adds an optional breed pre-qualifier phase before the main BIE elimination.
- Supports up to six BIE judges, with individual judge sheets, complete score entry, saved round history, and supplemental score-detail pages.
- Adds a printable BIE running-confirmation sheet and bulk controls to mark all eligible hounds running, not running, or undecided.
- Allows saved pre-qualifier and elimination rounds to be reopened for correction, then safely rebuilds later dependent rounds.
- Balances three-dog BIE draws to avoid unnecessary solo courses; ten hounds are drawn as 3, 3, 2, and 2.
- Prevents SQLite Undo and server-restored states from queuing a redundant save back into Undo history.

Retains the verified application updater and the signed, notarized macOS release workflow.
